# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

InfinityScale is a small, volunteer-maintained open-source project. We welcome
people who are respectful, specific, and willing to improve the work together.

Contributors are expected to:

- Be welcoming and constructive when discussing code, images, algorithms, and
  product decisions.
- Explain disagreement with evidence and trade-offs rather than personal
  attacks.
- Respect privacy, copyright, and the consent of people whose images or work
  are used in examples.
- Keep bug reports and pull requests reproducible and focused.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, doxxing, threats,
sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, malicious code, or publishing
private material without consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove content and may block participation when
behavior violates this policy. For a sensitive report, use GitHub's private
vulnerability reporting channel or contact the repository maintainer privately;
do not disclose private evidence in a public issue.

This policy applies in the repository, its issue tracker, discussions, pull
requests, and project-related public channels.
